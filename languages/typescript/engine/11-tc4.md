## Before TC4, step 0a as built: the cap on contexts per function (`c6b88b2`, `7021f7a`; reviewed 2026-11-26)

**What it is.** As decided (08-tc4 §0): core's `Limits::max_contexts_per_function: Option<u32>` (`None` by default and in `Limits::new`) and
`Cut::MaxContextsPerFunction` (`"max_contexts_per_function"`, after `MaxContexts`). `graph_within` counts per function the contexts it reads
(`Run::per_function`, raised right after `read += 1`); the checks run `max_contexts`, `max_depth`, the cap, the cancel. TypeScript:
`calls::CONTEXTS_PER_FUNCTION = 64` and `calls::limits()`, used by `Options::new` and by the measure's `solve_timed`; the tests' own
`Limits::default()` and `Limits::new(u32::MAX, None)` are unchanged.

**Tests.** Core, on a toy language with keys (`Fan`: `main` calls `f` under 1, 2, 3; `f` calls `g`):
`max_contexts_per_function_cuts_the_next_context_of_that_function`, `max_contexts_per_function_none_or_one_context_never_cuts` (also `Toy`'s
diamond at cap 1), `max_contexts_per_function_counts_reads_so_a_warm_memo_cuts_the_same` (the second graph equals the first in entries, defs,
contexts, edges and cuts; `walks` 4 then 0). TypeScript: the row `cap-per-function` (three contexts of `run`, nothing cut) and
`the_cap_per_function_cuts_the_third_context_of_run` (cap 2: `run`'s third context, the one `third` calls, is `Incomplete`, counted once; no
call reaches `c`, which `third` still hands over as a passed edge; both properties hold; cap 3 = no cap). The four `*-off.txt` snapshots gained
the new row's section (+2 lines each: they are written over every row of the table; the prompt missed that).
